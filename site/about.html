About Primecore

A business solutions partner, built around your work.

Primecore Technologies Limited brings technology, supplies, professional services and organisational development together for clients across Zambia.

Who we are

Helping organisations work with confidence.

Primecore Technologies Limited is a diversified Zambian business solutions company headquartered in Lusaka. We support government institutions, private companies, universities, NGOs, schools, construction businesses, SMEs and individual clients.

Our work connects the practical requirements organisations face every day: equipment and supplies, appropriate technology, safe and well-maintained workplaces, professional learning and business support.

We begin with the client’s context. Clear requirements, transparent scope and agreed next steps help create a dependable working relationship.

Our business approach

Start with the requirement. Agree the right response.

Each enquiry is considered in context so the proposed scope, specifications and next steps are clear.

Listen to the organisation’s goals, constraints and practical operating environment.
Clarify product specifications, service scope, quantities and delivery locations.
Discuss available options and what needs to be confirmed before a commitment.
Keep communication and responsibilities clear throughout the engagement.
Our commitments

Quality, responsibility and lasting value.

Commitment to quality

We aim to understand the specification, intended use and acceptance requirements before preparing a proposal. Product availability and service scope are confirmed with each quotation.

Commitment to sustainability

We recognise the importance of responsible resource use, safe workplaces and inclusive opportunity. Sustainability priorities are considered where they are relevant to each engagement.

Partnership in practice

We value constructive collaboration with clients, training institutions, community organisations and strategic partners, with each relationship described only as details are approved.

Appropriate expertise

Consultancy and training are distinct from regulated professional services that require specific licensing or accreditation. Any such requirement should be confirmed before engagement.
